## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-22

The first complete state: a world, seven lenses, and the ground truth to score against.

### Added
- **World.** 40 tables, roughly 38,000 rows, three years of causally coherent history,
  regenerated byte-identically from a seed and a fixed as-of date.
- **Seven lenses.** ServiceNow, Splunk, Onspring, Okta, Tenable, Workday and
  CrowdStrike, covering all seven connector patterns in DESIGN.md #5, plus framework
  and taxonomy sync and binary evidence retrieval.
- **Ground truth.** 1,217 expectations across attribution, conflict, degradation and
  absence, with 5,023 true evidence links and 773 deliberate traps.
- **Control Center.** Eight surfaces generated from the world, so the documentation
  cannot drift from what a connector actually reads, including an eight-chapter manual.
- **Harness.** 89 self-checks, a three-level chaos dial, real OAuth against Keycloak,
  and an adversarial WireMock proxy for the degradation family.
- **Provenance manifests.** Every twin records where its response shapes came from.
  All seven are `unverified`.

### Security
- Every published port binds `127.0.0.1`. Docker's short port form binds `0.0.0.0`,
  and the Postgres role is the image's bootstrap superuser.
- Host directories are mounted read-only.
- Attachment download tokens are HMAC-keyed independently of the bearer token.
- The Control Center renders no credential on any page.

## The world contract

A world is reproducible from five values:

```
(seed, as-of, scale, chaos, generator_version)
```

Given the same five, `world/generate.py` produces a byte-identical world, and therefore
identical ground truth. `generator_version` lives in `world_meta` and is visible on
`/healthz`.

**A change to `generator_version` invalidates every golden file.** Any change to the
generator that alters the sequence of random draws reshuffles the entire world, even
where the change looks local: adding a lens, reordering a loop, or changing how many
values a loop consumes will move counts in unrelated tables. That is expected and
recorded, not a bug.

- **Patch** releases do not touch the generator. Twins, the Control Center, docs and
  the harness may change. Golden files stay valid.
- **Minor** releases may add lenses, endpoints or tables. If `generator_version` bumps,
  the release notes say so and golden files must be rebaselined.
- **Major** releases may change the ground-truth schema or the scoring contract.

If you need a world to survive a generator change, snapshot it: `./scripts/snapshot
my-baseline` writes a restorable dump that is independent of the generator.
